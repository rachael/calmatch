    How is this private?
    • There's no server. This is a static page; everything runs in your browser.
    • Google access is read-only, used once, and revoked the moment your events are loaded. Nothing is saved — close the tab and it's gone.
    • The link you send holds only the date range and short scrambled fingerprints of your events — no titles, times, or details. It sits after the #, which browsers never send to any server.
    • Your friend's browser fingerprints their own events the same way and compares. Only events you both have can show up.
    • Fair warning: someone holding your link could test guesses ("was there an event called Dentist at 3pm on the 4th?"). Only send it to the person you're comparing with.
